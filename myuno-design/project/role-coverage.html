Read-out

Strong on consumer + owner. Weak on pro workspaces + investor.

Tourist and Owner were clearly built first and they show it. Resident is close behind but missing daily-frequency verticals (grocery, home maintenance, coworking). Agent and Provider have the core but lack the pro workspace shell. Developer is mid-build. Investor is the only role needing a full greenfield surface — today it's mostly calculator + advisory pages.
