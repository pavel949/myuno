## Business decisions to approve

These are proposals only, not defaults silently implemented. Registry IDs link to `p0a-contract-blockers.csv`.

1. **PC-01 pricing:** Рекомендую: approve one server quote, date override → applicable active season/rate plan → base, with explicit timing thresholds, one length discount plus eligible timing discount, fixed cleaning treatment and minor-unit rounding. This removes caller-selected price envelopes; approve overlap precedence and legacy JSON retirement separately. Current 10% deposit is a code fact, not newly approved policy.
2. **PC-02 override/on-behalf:** Рекомендую: keep negotiated/zero price, fee waivers and booking for another customer denied until existing protected approver duty, limits, reason and approval snapshot are specified. No role is granted by this proposal. Decide whether complimentary bookings are payable orders or separate unpaid operational requests.
3. **PC-03 manual receipt/FX:** Рекомендую: preserve protected platform-admin confirmation ingress for the first replacement, require evidence tied to an existing trusted order, and approve a protected fixed FX snapshot with explicit rounding/under/overpayment policy. Do not extend to managers/finance members implicitly; do not use browser estimates or fallback FX as evidence. Whether a company finance-edit duty may settle its own orders remains an explicit decision.
4. **PC-04 installment/reversal:** Рекомендую: record only verified received amounts, keep remaining receivable explicit, and defer full payment/commission/refund/inventory success until approved allocation and reconciliation. Decide fee/MC/owner recognition timing, partial payment tolerance, currency accounts, paid cancellation/refund policy and unpaid-confirmed PMS/operator acceptance semantics.
5. **PC-05 merchant/commission:** Рекомендую: derive org through protected existing links and snapshot approved commission agreements/management terms; block unmapped/mixed-merchant transactions. Decide common precedence and commission units before replacing divergent helpers.

Approval does not itself authorize deploy, direct-path revocation or GO-A. Next implementation needs exact isolated price/actor/receipt/replay/inventory compatibility tests and reviewed restrictive forward changes. No P0-B/E3 advancement.
