## Data provenance

- Do not promise returns or imply that a submitted estimate is verified performance.
- `expected_irr` remains an estimate supplied with a project. Never relabel it as historical/comparable IRR without separate sourced evidence.
- Calculator outputs depend on inputs and assumptions. A disclaimer must not assert those outputs are historical observations.
- Publish historical/comparable figures only with their actual source, period and calculation basis. Do not add dates to an unsupported citation.
- Keep uncertainty visible. A disclaimer cannot make an invented figure or claim acceptable.
